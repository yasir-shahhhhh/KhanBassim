About inAmigos Foundation

inAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered nonprofit under the Companies Act 2013, founded by Mr. Govind Shukla on September 23, 2020. The organization holds 80G & 12A certifications, CSR-1 registration, and is officially registered with NITI Aayog (NGO Darpan). Operating across multiple states in India, inAmigos combines grassroots volunteering, awareness campaigns, and skill-development initiatives to address systemic social challenges.
