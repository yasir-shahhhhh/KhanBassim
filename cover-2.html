Cover Design

PROTEIOS ROLLUP — Institutional Identity

Design Concept

This rollup cover was designed for the Proteios Education seminars. It uses the institutional color palette and bold typography to create a high-impact visual presence for physical events.

Visual Strategy

The layout emphasizes legibility and brand recognition from a distance. By layering the core identity marks over a clean, professional background, the design ensures that the Proteios mission is immediately communicated to every attendee.
