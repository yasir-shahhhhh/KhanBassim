About Proteios Education

Proteios Education is a modern education and youth development initiative operating in Kashmir, India. The organization works at the intersection of education, mentorship, career guidance, creativity, and real-world exposure — helping students go beyond traditional academics to develop the skills and mindset needed for the future.
