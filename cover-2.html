Cover Design

PROTEIOS EDUCATION - Institutional Rollup Banner

Concept

This cover is a rollup banner design created for Proteios Education, an ed-tech counseling startup. It serves as a physical marketing asset for events, workshops, and office branding, translating the digital brand identity into a tangible, print-ready format.

Design Psychology

The psychology is built on clarity as credibility. The headline "LESS NOISE. MORE CLARITY." is not just a tagline but a design principle applied throughout the banner itself. In a landscape where education companies overwhelm students with information overload, this banner deliberately uses negative space dominance, leaving over 40% of the canvas as breathing room. This spacious layout subconsciously communicates the brand's core promise: we simplify, not complicate. The dark charcoal background with subtle diagonal light streaks in teal/green creates a premium, corporate aesthetic that positions Proteios above typical coaching institute branding (which tends to be loud, colorful, and cluttered). The bullet-point service list (Career Counseling, Mentorship, Study Strategy, Global Opportunities, Workshops & Seminars) uses teal accent dots that match the logo's arrow motif, creating a consistent visual system. The Proteios logo with its upward-right arrow symbolizes forward momentum and aspiration, perfect for an education brand.

Technical Execution

The layout follows a strict vertical hierarchy optimized for standing-distance readability: logo at top (brand recognition), headline in large italic serif (emotional hook), subtitle in smaller weight (rational explanation), services list in mid-weight (information delivery), and website URL at bottom (call to action). The diagonal teal light effect in the lower-right corner breaks the otherwise rigid composition with organic movement, adding visual interest without compromising the minimalist aesthetic. The rollup banner mockup with chrome hardware adds photorealistic context, helping stakeholders visualize the final print product. Typography uses a premium serif for the headline and clean sans-serif for body text, creating a sophisticated typographic contrast.
