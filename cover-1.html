PORSCHE 911 GT3 RS — Automotive Cover

What it is

A premium automotive cover design built for performance branding. Created to showcase the Porsche 911 GT3 RS in a monochrome-green editorial layout that communicates speed, precision, and exclusivity.

How it was made

The composition uses a desaturated green palette with strategic contrast highlights to draw the eye to the car's aggressive front profile. Typography is positioned to balance the visual weight of the vehicle while maintaining clean editorial hierarchy. The poster-like proportions and border framing give it a magazine-quality identity suitable for both digital and print use.
