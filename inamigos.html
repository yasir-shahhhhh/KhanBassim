Internship Overview

During July 2025 – August 2025, Baasim Fayaz Khan served as a Graphic Design Intern for the inAmigos Foundation, a Section 8 registered nonprofit organization based in India.

His work focused on visual communication for social impact campaigns, helping the foundation reach thousands of beneficiaries through clean, high-impact digital designs and social media content.

Contributions

As part of a national creative team, Baasim deployed visual assets that aligned with the foundation's core mission of social empowerment. This role allowed him to master brand guidelines while working in a fast-paced, mission-driven environment.
