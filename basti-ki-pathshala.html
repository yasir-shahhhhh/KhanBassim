About Basti Ki Pathshala Foundation

Basti Ki Pathshala Foundation is a prominent Indian non-governmental organization (NGO) registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. The foundation works at the grassroots level to eradicate poverty and build a brighter future by providing quality primary education to children from slums and underprivileged backgrounds.

Educational Empowerment: Providing essential primary education, learning materials, and stationery kits to children lacking basic educational resources.
Youth Skill Internships: Offering structured work-from-home internship opportunities for college students across digital fundraising, web development, Python, and AI.
