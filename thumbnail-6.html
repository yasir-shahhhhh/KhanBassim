Thumbnail Design

HOW HE DID IT — Success Story Concept

What it is

A storytelling thumbnail designed for success analysis and case studies. It leverages curiosity-gap mechanics to drive engagement.

Creative Strategy

The design focuses on contrast and centered framing to create a sense of mystery and authority. By using a limited color palette of black, white, and a single accent color, the thumbnail maintains a clean, professional aesthetic while ensuring the primary hook— the "How" — remains the focus.
