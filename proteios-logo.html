Brand Identity

Proteios Education — Visual Language

Design Concept

The Proteios logo is a strategic blend of minimalist geometry and organic growth symbols. It was designed to represent the concept of "upward momentum" in education, using sharp lines to signify precision and a centered balance to represent institutional stability.

Color Palette

Built on a foundation of professional dark tones with vibrant growth-green accents, the identity communicates authority, innovation, and educational evolution.
