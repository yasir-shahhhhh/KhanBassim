About The WathHarbor Project

WathHarbor - a blend of the Kashmiri word 'Wath' meaning Path and the English word 'Harbor' meaning Destination - is a community-owned, crowdfunded private university currently being developed in Jammu and Kashmir. Founded by Dr. Mehboob Makhdoomi, the institution is based on public contributions rather than having individual private owners.

The primary goal of the project is to establish a world-class educational and research institution right in the heart of Kashmir, aiming to reverse brain drain and halt the massive out-migration of students.

Pattan Campus Land: WathHarbor acquired land in the Pattan area to establish a premier academic and research campus.
Entrepreneurial Hub: Featuring Kashmir's first research-based startup incubator for youth innovators.
High-Growth Fields: Focusing on Artificial Intelligence, robotics, tourism innovation, and horticulture technology.
