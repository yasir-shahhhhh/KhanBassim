About Me

Myself Baasim Fayaz Khan, a 17-year-old Class 11 Humanities student from Srinagar.

I’m the Chief Operating Officer (COO) at Proteios, a student-focused initiative - or as I like to call it, a Human Potential Accelerator. I’m also the Co-Founder & CEO of Vitasta, a platform to connect students, NGOs, schools, colleges, businesses, brands, companies, etc.

Proteios Initiative

I’ve also worked across operations, graphic design, thumbnail design, video editing, scriptwriting, and AI tools, and I’ve also done internships in graphic design at InAmigos Foundation, fundraising at Basti ki Pathshala, worked with MLA Pulwama, and visual storytelling association at Wath Harbor.

Over the last few years, I’ve explored many fields, met incredible people like Younus Beigh, Shafat Qazi, Khalid Wani, Dr. Shashi Tharoor, Nabeel Showkat, Dr. CL Vishen, and Dr. Altaf Lal, learned fast, and shaped my direction around youth opportunity and student growth.
