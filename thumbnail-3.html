YOU DON'T NEED ADS — Marketing Strategy

What it is

A business and marketing thumbnail featuring Alex Hormozi. It's designed to stop the scroll by presenting a contrarian view on advertising, appealing to entrepreneurs and marketers looking for organic growth secrets.

How it was made

I utilized a high-resolution portrait of Alex Hormozi to leverage his personal brand authority. The headline 'YOU DON'T NEED ADS' uses a clean, heavy font with 'ADS' underlined in red to draw maximum focus to the subject of the video. The '@AlexHormozi' handle was included with a blue checkmark to further verify the content's authenticity. I added a subtle vignette and background texture to make the text pop against the white-grey section.
