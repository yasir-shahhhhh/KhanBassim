PHYSICS — Concept & Legacy Spotlight

What it is

An educational thumbnail centered around Ernest Rutherford, built for topic-intro and concept-explainer content in science education.

How it was made

The design uses a grayscale portrait with selective red and cyan accents to establish authority and visual hierarchy. Supporting symbols like the atom icon, wave-form sketch, and periodic table layer reinforce subject context while the red title treatment drives immediate recognition.
