PROTEIOS EDUCATION — Institutional Rollup Cover

What it is

An institutional rollup banner cover designed for Proteios Education to establish brand presence at events, workshops, and campus outreach programs.

How it was made

The layout follows a clean vertical hierarchy emphasizing clarity, trust, and student-first communication. Blue tones and structured typography create an approachable yet professional atmosphere. The design balances institutional credibility with youthful energy to resonate with students while maintaining organizational authority.
