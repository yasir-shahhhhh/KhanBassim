KASHMIR GREAT LAKES — Travel Documentary

What it is

A cinematic travel thumbnail designed for documentary storytelling around Kashmir’s iconic alpine lake routes.

How it was made

The layout uses panoramic composition to emphasize scale and natural depth, while oversized bottom typography ensures instant topic recognition even at small YouTube preview sizes. Saturation and clarity were pushed in the water and sky tones to create a crisp expedition feel.
