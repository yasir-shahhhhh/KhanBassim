About Me

Myself Baasim Fayaz Khan, a 17-year-old Class 11 Humanities student from Srinagar.

I’m the Chief Operating Officer (COO) at Proteios, a student-focused initiative — or as I like to call it, a Human Potential Accelerator. I’m also the Co-Founder & CEO of Vitasta, a platform connecting students, NGOs, schools, colleges, businesses, brands, and companies through opportunities.

Proteios Initiative

I’ve worked across operations, graphic design, thumbnail design, video editing, scriptwriting, and AI tools. My journey includes internships in graphic design at InAmigos Foundation, fundraising at Basti ki Pathshala, public policy research with the MLA Pulwama, and visual storytelling at WathHarbor.

Over the last few years, I’ve explored many fields, met incredible leaders like Younus Beigh, Shafat Qazi, Khalid Wani, Dr. Shashi Tharoor, Dr. CL Vishen, and Dr. Altaf Lal, learned fast, and shaped my direction around youth opportunity and student growth.

Alongside building, I’ve written articles for regional newspapers and document my thoughts, journey, and projects through my page, BaasimUnfiltered.
